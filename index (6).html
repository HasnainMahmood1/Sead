Event Objectives

WHAT WE'RE
BUILDING TOWARD.

01
Objective One

ACADEMIC APPLICATION

Students apply their classroom learning in a hands-on project environment, building solutions that connect STEM education to real-world problems across coding, design, and creative thinking.

Full Timeline

THE PROGRAM.

7:30

Arrival

All participants arrive and get settled before the day begins.

7:45

Opening Ceremony

The event structure and rules are formally presented. Teams are confirmed and the day ahead is outlined.

8:15

Coding Session 1

8:15 – 10:00. The first dedicated development session. Teams begin building their projects in HTML using pre-installed VS Code. Beginner support is available throughout.

10:00

Break

10:00 – 10:30. A scheduled break for participants to rest and recharge.

10:30

Coding Session 2

Development continues. Teams build on their morning progress, expanding features and refining their projects.

12:30

Disqualification Disclaimer

A disclaimer regarding disqualification criteria is given to all participants.

1:00

Disqualification Review

Participants who are not at least halfway done or have used fully AI-generated work are announced for disqualification.

1:00

Coding Session 3

1:00 – 3:00. The final development session. Teams complete their builds, finalize code, and prepare for submission by uploading to GitHub.

3:00

Judgement

3:00 – 3:15. Projects are judged by the organizers. In the meantime, participants move to the auditorium to prepare for the closing ceremony.

3:15

Closing Ceremony & Prizes

3:15 – 4:00. The day concludes in the auditorium. Prize winners are announced and every participant is celebrated for what they built.

Frequently Asked Questions

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Who can participate? +

New World International School students in Grades 8–12, according to the event proposal. The event is exclusively for the school community.

What language are we coding in? +

HTML. All projects are built in HTML, making the event accessible to beginners while still giving experienced coders room to work.

What development software will be available? +

Pre-installed VS Code will be available on machines at the event. Participants do not need to set anything up in advance.

How do I submit my project? +

Upload your project to GitHub and submit the repository link through the official Google Form. The submission form will be shared on the day.

Is support available if I'm a beginner? +

Yes. Zoom support is planned specifically for beginners throughout the event. You won't be left without help.

Where does SEAD take place? +

Coding sessions take place in classrooms at New World International School. Ceremonies are held in the auditorium.

Is Wi-Fi available? +

School Wi-Fi will be provided for all participants throughout the event.
